Commission a Relic

Have an idea for a dice tower, a set of terrain for your campaign, or a one-of-a-kind gift? Here's how a custom print comes together.

  1. 1

    Tell us the idea

    Send a description, sketches or reference images, plus size, colours and any deadline.

  2. 2

    Get a quote

    We'll reply with options, a price estimate and a timeline — usually within a couple of days.

  3. 3

    Design & preview

    We model or source the design and share a preview so you can request tweaks before printing.

  4. 4

    Print & deliver

    Your piece is printed, cleaned up and finished, then shipped or arranged for local pickup.

The Maker

Placeholder copy — Chris, replace this section with your story.

Layered Relics started the way most good quests do: with a game night, a dice tower that wasn't quite right, and a 3D printer humming in the corner. A few prototypes later, friends were asking for their own.

Today every piece is designed or hand-picked, printed in small batches and cleaned up by hand. The goal is simple — make things that feel like treasure pulled from a dungeon, but sturdy enough to survive the table.

If you can imagine it, there's a good chance it can be printed. Let's make something mythic.

— Chris Bowen, Layered Relics
